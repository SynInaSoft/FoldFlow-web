FoldFlow Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 13, 2026

FoldFlow (“the app”) does not collect personal information. This page explains how the app handles data.

1. Information we do not collect

The app has no sign-up or login. It does not collect or transmit your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information. It contains no analytics SDKs and no advertising identifiers, and it does not track you across apps or websites.

2. Information stored on your device

What you enter in the app is stored only on your device and is never sent anywhere:

  • Task (panel) names and checklist steps
  • Timer and schedule settings
  • Quick task lists
  • Saved favorite processes and groups
  • Archived tasks
  • App settings such as notifications and sort order

Deleting the app deletes this data with it. The “Export data” feature in Settings only runs when you choose to save or share a file yourself; the app never transmits anything on its own.

3. Notifications

Timer and schedule alerts are local notifications scheduled on your device. Their contents never pass through a server, and the app does not use remote push notifications. You can revoke notification permission at any time in your device settings.

4. In-app purchases

FoldFlow Pro is purchased through Apple's App Store payment system on iPhone and iPad, or Google Play Billing on Android, with purchase state managed by RevenueCat. The app never accesses your payment details or your Apple or Google account information — only an anonymous identifier and purchase history used to confirm whether you own Pro. This information is not used to identify you personally, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.

  • Apple Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy: revenuecat.com/privacy

5. Children's privacy

Because the app collects no personal information, it collects no personal information from children either.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated text and revision date will be posted on this page.

7. Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us at syninasoft@gmail.com.
