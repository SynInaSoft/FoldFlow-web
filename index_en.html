FoldFlow

Your multitasking helper — several things, one screen

FoldFlow is a multitasking helper for people who keep several things moving at once. Multitasking isn't hard because there's a lot to do — it's hard because there's no way to see everything at the same time. FoldFlow holds up to five tasks on one screen: while you focus on one, the rest fold into narrow bars. Each bar is its own gauge — the height of the colored fill is the time remaining, so a glance tells you where everything stands.

Features

  • Folding task panels — switch between tasks with a tap or a swipe
  • Gauge timers — see the time left without reading numbers
  • One-handed adjustment — press and drag a digit or the gauge edge
  • Scheduling — set something for tomorrow morning or two days out
  • Quick tasks — jot down small errands and attach an alarm
  • Step-by-step checklists, saved as reusable favorites
  • Follow-up reminders if you don't come back when time is up

No account, no cloud. Everything stays on your device.
